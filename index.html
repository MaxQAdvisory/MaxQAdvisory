SERVICES

Four ways to work together

Founder Strategy Session

90 MIN

A focused session on the decisions that matter most right now: priorities, funding mix, go-to-market and where to spend limited time and money. Each founder leaves with a clear direction and practical next steps.

Strategy Note

ADD-ON

A written plan following a strategy session, setting out the agreed priorities, recommended actions and relevant introductions, ready to share with a board or co-founders.

Fractional Commercial Leadership

1 DAY / WEEK

Ongoing commercial leadership as an advisor acting as fractional CCO. Commercial strategy, opportunity identification, programmes and events worth pursuing, market and sector intelligence, and building and hiring the sales function, so the technical team can stay focused on the product.

A behind-the-scenes advisory role focused on strategy and the commercial engine.

Focused Projects

DEFINED SCOPE

Defined pieces of commercial work with a clear scope and outcome, such as investor readiness and data room preparation, a go-to-market plan or a partnership strategy.

Fees are tailored to each company's stage and requirements.
